What is Nova Helix?

Runs entirely on your machine

Nova Helix is a desktop AI assistant powered by language models running locally on your own hardware. No account required to chat, no server round-trips, no usage surveillance.

Private by design

Your conversations, memories, and files stay on your computer. What you tell Nova Helix is yours — it never leaves your machine.

A companion, not a chatbox

Nova Helix remembers what matters to you, keeps context across sessions, and shows you exactly what it's doing while it thinks, searches, and writes.

It remembers you

Persistent memory with semantic recall — it finds what matters by meaning, not just keywords. Erase it any time; erased means erased.

Honest by design

It tells you what it doesn't know instead of making things up, and cites its sources when it searches.

Built for real hardware

The Free tier runs on standard laptops. Higher tiers scale up to flagship-class local models for high-end GPUs.

Under the hood

Nova Helix is commercial software, but here is the short version of how it is built.

Rust + CUDA inference engine

A custom high-performance engine, with a CPU path tuned so the Free tier genuinely runs on ordinary laptops.

Native desktop app

Built with Flutter for a fast, themeable desktop presence rather than a browser window in disguise.

Offline licence verification

Your licence works without phoning home — consistent with everything else staying on your machine.

Signed updates

Updates are cryptographically signed, so you can verify what you are installing.
