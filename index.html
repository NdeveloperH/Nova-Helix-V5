What is Nova Helix?

Runs entirely on your machine

Nova Helix is a desktop AI assistant powered by language models running locally on your own hardware. No account required to chat, no server round-trips, no usage surveillance.

Private by design

Your conversations, memories, and files stay on your computer. What you tell Nova Helix is yours — it never leaves your machine.

A companion, not a chatbox

Nova Helix remembers what matters to you, keeps context across sessions, and shows you exactly what it's doing while it thinks, searches, and writes.
