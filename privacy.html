Privacy Policy

Nova Helix — last updated 14 August 2026

The short version: Nova Helix runs entirely on your own computer. Your conversations, memories, files, and screen contents never leave your machine and are never sent to us. The only personal data we ever hold is what is needed to take a payment — and that is handled by Stripe, not by us.

1. What the application collects

Nothing. Nova Helix performs its AI processing locally using models stored on your device. Specifically:

  • Your chat messages and the assistant's replies stay on your device.
  • Stored memories, observations, and any screen or activity information the assistant uses stay on your device.
  • No usage analytics, telemetry, or behavioural tracking is transmitted to us.
  • No account is required to install or use the free tier.

If you enable an optional feature that fetches information from the internet, the request goes directly from your computer to that website. We do not proxy, log, or see it.

2. What we collect when you subscribe

If you buy a paid tier, payment is processed by Stripe, Inc. We receive from Stripe only:

  • your email address;
  • which tier you bought and whether the subscription is active;
  • a Stripe customer and subscription identifier.

We never receive or store your full card number. Card details are entered on Stripe's own payment page and handled under their security standards. See Stripe's privacy policy.

3. Why we hold it and for how long

Your email and subscription status are used only to issue your licence, deliver it to you, and honour renewals, cancellations, and support requests. Records of purchases are retained while your subscription is active and afterwards where required for accounting and tax purposes. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties for marketing.

4. Software updates

The application may check whether a newer version exists. This check reveals only that some installation asked; it does not transmit your identity or your data, and updates are never installed without your action.

5. Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Deleting your data ends any active subscription, since we can no longer identify your licence. To make a request, or to ask any privacy question, contact us via our GitHub page.

Data held on your own computer is yours alone: you can erase the application's memories at any time from within it, and uninstalling removes its local data.

6. Children

Nova Helix is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
